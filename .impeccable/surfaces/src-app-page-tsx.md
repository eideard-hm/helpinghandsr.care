# Home page

Scope: `/` (home), with `/testimonials` and the 404 page inheriting the same world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

## Audience and job

People in Abu Dhabi deciding whether to let a therapist come to them: professionals with pain or stress, athletes, hotel guests, wellness and aesthetics clients. They judge credibility, pick an hour or a treatment, and open WhatsApp with a ready message. The owner's steer: the idea to sell is the home visit itself.

## Proof and content

Richard's credentials (MOFA and KHDA attestation, Newton Training Center, 20+ years), the ZeinMotion Therapy method, the visible treatments, real session footage, ten FAQ answers. No prices, ratings or counts. The single testimonial is unverified and stays unmarked.

## Constraints

Logo as supplied. Green #2f7d6d and indigo #2c2d84 are binding. Section ids (`benefits`, `services`, `about`, `how-it-works`, `testimonials`, `faq`), the H1 text, structured data and the e2e suite stay working.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is one architectural section cut through an Abu Dhabi home across a day; the visit is read by where the light falls and which hour is stamped. It refuses the wellness template of photo hero, headline, button and a row of benefit cards.

OWN-WORLD: Limewash ground; indigo as section poche for cut walls, slabs, the ground and the footer; the logo's gold as the travelling sunbeam and the only active mark; green for the action and living things; a pale indigo shadow tint for withdrawn regions. Inclined drafted lettering in tracked caps with tabular time stamps, sharp rectangular controls, leader lines, dimension ticks, schedules and a title block; photographs set as apertures in indigo reveals that come into colour when the light reaches them.

STORY: The visitor recognises a home like theirs, understands that a credentialed therapist arrives at the door with the table, chooses an hour, and sends the WhatsApp message already written.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns on limewash: the H1 in three lines of inclined caps, the promise line, the hour control (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night) and the green WhatsApp action. Right seven columns, bleeding off the edge: the apartment in section, with Richard in the corridor at the door carrying the folded table, a dashed outline by the window where the table goes, the gold beam at the chosen hour, stamped annotations and a dimension line reading "Your commute, 0 km".

FORM: Daylight section, catalog id architecture-inhabitable-space-daylight-section, a dealt challenger taken on the owner's steer over grounded candidate 5; seed key cd7d9a38. Signature interaction: choosing the hour moves the sun through the section and rewrites the WhatsApp message; the preferred hour joins the message only after the visitor has actively chosen one. Motion grammar: only light moves, with one sweep on arrival.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
